---
title: Planning a one-day session?
summary: Do these before the day so nothing waits on someone else.
---

Some partners run a one-day intensive: come in the morning with your SOC 2 done and leave with your package built and your Marketplace listing submitted. A few steps have wait time, so do them ahead. This view lists every task marked "do ahead".

The listing needs your trust center's public CPO link, so it happens on the day. FedRAMP IDs usually arrive in 3–5 business days, sometimes 2, so plan a short follow-up to add the ID to your CPO and submit (CA-16) inside the 7-day package freshness window (FRC-APP-FCP).

Also book your Sponsor for end-of-day solution capability sign-off (CA-12.4) and for the follow-up submission (CA-16.3).
