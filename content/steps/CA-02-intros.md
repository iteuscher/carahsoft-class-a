## Paramify

Paramify is a risk management platform, and Class A is at its core a risk-mapping exercise: take the security decisions your existing assessment validated, map them to FedRAMP's rule subset, and publish the result in a form a reviewer and a machine can both read. More CSPs have used Paramify to get on the FedRAMP marketplace than any other GRC platform.

## How this walkthrough works

Paramify provides the software, the videos, and this walkthrough. Your partner guides you and answers your questions. Follow the steps, fill out everything you need in Paramify, and submit your Class A with confidence.

## You submit, not your partner

> Providers MUST NOT use a third party to apply for a FedRAMP Certification on their behalf; this includes independent assessment services.
>
