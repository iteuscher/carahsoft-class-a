This walkthrough takes you from your existing assessment to a submitted FedRAMP 20x Class A certification. Work through the steps in order. Each step tells you what to do, where to do it, how you know you're done, and which FedRAMP rule it satisfies.

## Phases and the gate

The steps are grouped into three phases, shown in the step list on the left (or under the menu button on a phone).

| Phase | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Phase 1: Before your workspace | Prepare: choose your path, name your team, set up your FedRAMP Security Inbox, and gather your public information. No Paramify login needed. |
| Gate | Paramify provisions your workspace. Everything after this happens in Paramify. |
| Phase 2: In Paramify | Upload your materials, review solution capabilities, build your package, publish it to your trust center, and submit. |
| Phase 3: Ongoing | What you keep doing after you're certified. |

## Choices that tailor the walkthrough

- **Your path.** In CA-03 you choose the framework of your most recent assessment: SOC 2 Type II, GovRAMP, or FedRAMP Rev5. Tasks and FedRAMP wording change to match. You can change it any time in CA-03.
- **Your team.** In CA-04 you type a name for each role. From then on, tasks show the person's name instead of the role, so everyone can see what they own.
- **Show.** The Show menu in the header narrows every step to one person's or one role's tasks.
- **Platform choices.** Some steps ask one more question, such as which email platform runs your inbox in CA-05, and hide steps that don't apply.

## Forms

Some steps have forms instead of a plain checklist. In CA-06 and CA-10 you fill in your Certification Package Overview (CPO) directly on the page. Tasks next to a form check themselves when the form is complete. You can copy or download your answers to send to your Paramify contact.

## Where your progress is saved

Checked tasks, names, and form answers are saved in this browser only. There are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere. Use the same browser each time; clearing your browser data clears your progress.

## Getting help

Every step ends with **Send feedback**. It builds a short message for you to paste into a Paramify support request. Your partner and Paramify contacts are listed in CA-04.

Planning a one-day session? The **Workshop** view lists every task to do ahead of time.
